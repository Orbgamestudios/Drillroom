# Distinctions — one line each. Bold = the one question to ask.

## P/S

**Learning (7C)**
- Reinforcement vs punishment: **behavior up or down?** Up = reinforcement. Down = punishment.
- Positive vs negative: **added or taken away?** "Negative" never means bad.
- Classical vs operant: **automatic or chosen?** Salivate, flinch, feel anxious = classical. Press, study, call = operant.
- Fixed vs variable: **predictable or not?**
- Ratio vs interval: **counted by actions or by time?** Variable ratio = hardest to stop.
- Extinction vs spontaneous recovery: **faded, or came back after a rest?**
- Dissonance vs self-perception: **strong prior opinion?** Yes = dissonance. No = self-perception.
- Central vs peripheral route: **persuaded by the argument, or by looks and credentials?**
- Foot-in-the-door vs door-in-the-face: **small ask first, or huge ask first?**

**Social influence and groups (7B)**
- Conformity vs compliance vs obedience: **what prompted it?** Nothing = conformity. A request = compliance. An order = obedience.
- Normative vs informational: **to fit in, or because they think the group is right?**
- Facilitation vs loafing: **better with an audience, or slacking in a pooled group?**
- Groupthink vs polarization: **doubt silenced, or group drifted extreme?**
- Deindividuation: **lost sense of self in a crowd.**
- Bystander effect: **more witnesses, less help.**

**Status and roles (8C)**
- Ascribed vs achieved: **born with it or earned?**
- Master status: **the one others see first.**
- Role strain vs role conflict: **one role or two?** One = strain. Two = conflict.
- Primary vs secondary group: **emotional and lasting, or task and impersonal?**
- Reference group: **the group you compare yourself to.**
- Front stage vs back stage: **audience or not?**
- Individual vs institutional discrimination: **one person's act, or a rule?**

**Attribution (8B)**
- FAE vs actor-observer vs self-serving: **one person or two?** Two compared = actor-observer. One other person = FAE. Yourself by outcome = self-serving.
- Just-world: **victims get blamed because "people get what they deserve."**

**Memory and attention (6B)**
- Proactive vs retroactive interference: **old blocks new, or new blocks old?** Pro = old moves forward.
- Recall vs recognition: **cue in front of you?** Cue = recognition.
- Short-term vs working: **holding, or doing something with it?**
- Primacy vs recency: **first items or last?**
- Semantic vs episodic vs procedural: **fact, event you lived, or skill?**
- Broca vs Wernicke: **broken speech that makes sense, or fluent nonsense?**
- Selective vs divided attention: **one thing or two?**
- Heuristic vs algorithm: **shortcut or guaranteed method?**
- Mental set vs functional fixedness: **reusing a method, or seeing an object only its usual way?**

**Sensation (6A)**
- Sensation vs perception: **detect or interpret?**
- Absolute vs difference threshold: **can you detect it, or notice it changed?** Weber: change is a percentage.
- Adaptation vs habituation: **receptors quit, or you learned to ignore it?**
- Bottom-up vs top-down: **pieces to picture, or expectation to picture?**
- Rods vs cones: **dim and periphery, or color and detail?**
- Sensitivity vs bias: **can they tell the difference, or just quick to say yes?**

**Biology, motivation, disorders (7A)**
- Drive-reduction vs incentive vs arousal: **remove a need, chase a reward, or reach the right alertness?**
- Intrinsic vs extrinsic: **for itself or for a reward?**
- Psychoanalytic vs humanistic vs behaviorist vs social cognitive vs trait: **hidden conflict, blocked growth, consequences, beliefs plus environment, or stable dimensions?**
- Schizophrenia positive vs negative: **something added, or something missing?**
- Bipolar I vs II: **full mania ever?** Yes = I.
- Panic vs GAD vs phobia: **attacks, constant worry, or one trigger?**
- Hypothalamus vs thalamus: **drives and hormones, or sensory relay?**
- Hippocampus vs amygdala: **new memories, or fear?**
- Sympathetic vs parasympathetic: **fight or flight, or rest and digest?**

**Identity (8A)**
- Self-efficacy vs self-esteem: **one task, or you overall?**
- Locus of control: **outcomes from me, or from luck and others?**
- Cooley vs Mead: **yourself through others' eyes, or "I" vs "me"?**
- Erikson vs Kohlberg vs Piaget vs Vygotsky: **life stages, moral reasoning, thinking ability, learning from skilled others.**
- Social identity theory: **self-worth from your groups.**

**Social structure (9A)**
- Functionalism vs conflict vs symbolic interactionism vs constructionism: **what is society?** Parts for stability. A power fight. Meanings in interaction. An agreement.
- Manifest vs latent function: **intended or unintended?**
- Sick role: **excused from duties, must try to get well.**
- Medicalization: **normal state becomes a medical condition.**
- Material vs symbolic culture: **objects or ideas?**
- Values vs beliefs vs norms: **important, true, or the rules?**
- Folkways vs mores vs taboos: **manners, morals, or unthinkable?**
- Culture lag vs culture shock: **ideas behind the tech, or lost in a new culture?**

**Demographics (9B)**
- Fertility vs fecundity: **actual births or possible births?**
- Mortality vs morbidity: **deaths or illness?**
- Demographic transition: **high-high, deaths fall, births fall, low-low.**
- Push vs pull: **driven out or drawn in?**
- Gentrification vs urban renewal: **private money moving in, or government redevelopment?**
- Relative deprivation: **feeling worse off compared to others.**
- Wide-base pyramid: **young, fast-growing.**

**Inequality (10A)**
- Class vs status vs power: **money, prestige, or control?**
- Social vs cultural capital: **who you know, or what you know how to do?**
- Absolute vs relative poverty: **can't meet needs, or poor compared to others?**
- Social reproduction: **inequality passed to the next generation.**
- Intersectionality: **overlapping identities, distinct disadvantage.**

## C/P

**5D Biological molecules**
- Enantiomers vs diastereomers: **all centers flipped, or some?**
- Epimers: **diastereomers differing at one center.**
- Anomers: **epimers at the ring-closing carbon.** Alpha = OH opposite CH2OH. Beta = same side.
- D/L vs R/S: **drawing convention vs absolute configuration. Unrelated to each other and to rotation.**
- Aldose vs ketose: **aldehyde or ketone sugar?**
- Saturated vs unsaturated: **no double bonds and solid, or double bonds and liquid?**
- pKa vs pI: **one group, or the whole molecule at zero net charge?**
- Primary vs secondary vs tertiary vs quaternary: **sequence, local folds, whole chain, several chains.**

**5A Acids and buffers**
- Strong vs weak: **fully dissociates or partly?**
- Buffer range: **within one pH unit of pKa.**
- Half-equivalence vs equivalence: **pH = pKa, or moles matched?** Equivalence isn't pH 7 unless both strong.
- Henderson-Hasselbalch: **pH = pKa + log(base/acid).**

**5B Forces and separations**
- Hydrogen bond: **H on N, O, or F.**
- Boiling point: **stronger forces up, branching down.**
- Distillation vs extraction vs chromatography vs recrystallization: **boiling point, solubility and charge, polarity, temperature solubility.**

**5E Thermo and kinetics**
- Thermo vs kinetics: **will it happen, or how fast?**
- Catalyst: **lowers activation energy only. ΔG and K unchanged.**
- ΔG° vs ΔG: **standard conditions, or actual concentrations?**
- Q vs K: **where you are, or where you end up.** Q < K = forward.
- Spontaneous: **not the same as fast.**

**4A Motion and energy**
- Work: **needs force along the motion.**
- Kinetic vs potential: **motion or position?**
- Conservative vs non-conservative: **energy recoverable (gravity, springs) or lost (friction)?**
- Static vs kinetic friction: **up to a max, or one value?**
- Mass vs weight: **amount or force?**

**4D Light and sound**
- Converging vs diverging: **real inverted image possible, or always virtual upright small?**
- Refraction: **bends toward normal into denser medium.**
- Decibels: **+10 dB = 10 times intensity.**
- Doppler: **approaching = higher pitch.**

**4E Atoms and decay**
- Alpha vs beta-minus vs positron vs gamma: **mass down 4, Z up 1, Z down 1, no change.**
- Half-life: **(1/2)^n remains.**
- Quantum numbers: **n shell, l shape, ml orientation, ms spin.**

## B/B

**1A Enzymes**
- Competitive vs noncompetitive vs uncompetitive: **Km up Vmax same, Vmax down Km same, both down.**
- Cooperativity: **sigmoidal curve.**

**1B Genetics**
- Transcription vs translation: **DNA to RNA, or RNA to protein?**
- Missense vs nonsense vs silent vs frameshift: **one amino acid, early stop, no change, everything downstream.**
- Codominance vs incomplete dominance: **both fully, or a blend?**

**1D Metabolism**
- Glycolysis: **cytoplasm, no oxygen, 2 ATP net.**
- TCA and ox phos: **mitochondria, need oxygen.**
- Insulin vs glucagon: **store or release?**
- Fatty acids: **most ATP per gram.**

**2A Cells and membranes**
- Simple vs facilitated diffusion: **no protein, or protein, both downhill.**
- Primary vs secondary active transport: **ATP directly, or a gradient made by ATP?**
- Mitosis vs meiosis I vs meiosis II: **identical copies, homologs split, sisters split.**
- Crossing over: **prophase I.**

**3A Nervous and endocrine**
- Peptide vs steroid hormones: **surface receptor and fast, or enters cell and slow?**
- Anterior vs posterior pituitary: **makes its own, or releases hypothalamus's (ADH, oxytocin)?**

**3B Organ systems**
- Bohr effect: **more CO2 or lower pH, hemoglobin lets go of O2.**
- Filtration vs reabsorption vs secretion: **out at glomerulus, back in, pushed out.**
- Innate vs adaptive: **fast and general, or slow, specific, with memory?**
- Humoral vs cell-mediated: **B cells and antibodies, or T cells killing infected cells?**
